03About

Two years owning the backend, end to end.

I finished my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at California State University, Fresno, where I spent two years as a research assistant to Prof. Aaron Stillmaker, chair of the ECE department.

I am most at home between synthesis and signoff: the stretch where a netlist gets a floorplan, a power grid, a clock tree and wires, and then has to meet timing at every corner. I work in both the commercial stack industry signs off with and the open flow that let me take a chip to tapeout on my own.
