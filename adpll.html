All-digital PLL on SkyWater SKY130

GDSII submitted · Sept 7, 2026Sole designer · Aug – Sept 2026

Key results

standard cells

40MHz

timing closed

15/15

prechecks, first try

A phase-locked loop turns a slow, clean reference clock into a faster one that stays locked to it. An all-digital PLL replaces the analog charge pump and filter with logic, so the whole thing can be built from standard cells and pushed through a normal digital flow.

  • Designed the loop in synthesizable Verilog: counter-based time-to-digital phase detector, digital PI loop filter, standard-cell ring-oscillator DCO with a tap mux, programmable ÷N feedback, and an averaging lock detector.
  • Hardened through OpenLane/OpenROAD on SKY130 and closed timing at 40 MHz with sub-10% IR drop. DRC, LVS and antenna clean in Magic and KLayout; all 15 Tiny Tapeout prechecks passed on the first attempt.
  • Found and fixed an out-of-range DCO tap index that left half the taps undefined, so the ring could not oscillate at its operating point. Resized the chain from the tap count to 32 inverters and quantized the behavioural model to the same 16 taps.
  • Verified in cocotb with Icarus Verilog: locks at about 39.81 MHz for N = 4 from the 10 MHz reference, in roughly 650 µs of simulated time.
