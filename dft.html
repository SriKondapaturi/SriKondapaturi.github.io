DFT, BIST and ATPG flow with Synopsys TetraMAX

Scan · BIST · ATPGSole designer · Aug 2024 – Feb 2025

Key results

Design-for-test adds hardware so a chip can be checked after it comes back from the fab. Scan chains let a tester shift a value into every flip-flop and read the result out; built-in self-test lets the chip generate and check its own patterns.

  • Integrated scan chains and BIST (LFSR/MISR) into an FSM controller through DFT-aware synthesis, then generated ATPG patterns in TetraMAX to reach 90.93% stuck-at fault coverage.
  • Stitched and balanced the scan chains with test-mode timing checked in both shift and capture, and validated the architecture across RTL and gate-level simulation.
